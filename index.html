Innovative Inserts

Unique Features of Our Arch Wing Insert:

Reversible

It's reversible - it can be used in either right or left shoe. Letters R/L faces upwards

Reduce Arch Collapse

Our Arch Wing has a high instep flange for more keystone support, to reduce arch collapse.

Greater Comfort

The heel extension reaches the full length of the arch, thus allowing greater comfort through the day.

How to install the Our Arch Wing Insert:

Installation in three easy steps

1

First Step:

Bend the Our Arch Wing Insert on the fold line to resemble a taco.
2

Second Step:

Place Insert in the shoe along the inside wall. The Insert should be folded up along-side the wall. The indicating letter R/L should be facing upwards.
3

Third Step:

Use the insole material provided to add more cushioning inside of the shoe, but the included insole is not required.
4

How it should look:

The Insert should sit flush in your shoe.

Our Foot-Care Project

Our Foot-Care Project is a humanitarian organization meant to help people who are experiencing painful feet due to overuse walk without pain again

Introduction

My interest in helping people walk goes back to my childhood.

My interest in helping people with disabilities began at a young age after seeing pictures of crippled children in a magazine.

My career started at a vocational school where I learned the shoe repair trade. While working as a shoe repair person I began studying Orthopedic Shoe Technology (the study of prescription shoe modifications). Then to the study of Pedorthics (the design, manufacture, modification and fit of footwear, shoes and foot orthoses to alleviate problems caused by disease, overuse, congenital condition or injury). Which led to the study of Orthotics (the design, manufacture, modification and fit of braces). My titles include: Shoemaker – OST, Orthopedic Shoe Technician - C.Ped., Certified Pedorthist – Registered Orthotic Technician – and CO, Certified Orthotist.

After many years designing, fabricating and fitting braces, my career came to an end. I had been diagnosed with a brain tumor and needed immediate surgery to remove the Schwannoma pressing on my brain stem. Some of the tumor had to be radiated because it was too close to my jugular vein for resection. The good news was that it was benign but I had lost my hearing on one side. The recovery was long; I had difficulties swallowing, speaking, right arm weakness and balance problems. I was 59.

One day I was watching my husband doing a remodeling project. I have always been amazed by his talent achieved through many years of experience as a carpenter. I missed my career and I needed a project. We often had conversations about our work and found there were similarities.

Some of my most creative thoughts have occurred while standing in front of a grinder. Over the years I have spent a lot of time: cutting, shaping, trimming, beveling, gluing, ungluing, padding and grinding patient’s shoes and orthotics to relieve discomfort and help them walk. The Arch Wing insert is a result of that experience and Our Footcare Project is our purpose.
